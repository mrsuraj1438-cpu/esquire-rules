Esquire-Style: 100 Rules for Men Never Give Up

Self-Respect & Confidence (1-10)

  • Stand tall — good posture speaks confidence.
  • Keep your word — reliability builds trust.
  • Never beg for validation.
  • Speak clearly — mumbling undermines authority.
  • Own at least one well-tailored suit.
  • Shine your shoes — details are noticed.
  • Stay active — don’t skip the gym.
  • Avoid oversharing online.
  • Control your intoxication — dignity first.
  • Keep your home presentable for guests.

Style & Grooming (11-30)

  • Wear clothes that fit — fit beats brand.
  • Choose a signature cologne used sparingly.
  • Maintain your hairstyle — intentional, not messy.
  • Keep fingernails clean and trimmed.
  • Don’t wear athletic sneakers with formal wear.
  • Iron your clothes — wrinkles aren’t character.
  • Wear a fresh undershirt.
  • Neutral color palette first (navy, black, white, gray).
  • Match belt leather with shoes.
  • Invest in quality basics — fit and fabric over trends.
  • Replace worn socks, underwear, and essentials regularly.
  • Sunglasses are a subtle style upgrade.
  • Keep watches and accessories minimal and purposeful.
  • Dress appropriately for weather — comfort + style.
  • Button jacket only when standing, never when sitting.
  • Learn a few tie knots — Windsor, half Windsor, four-in-hand.
  • Shoes should make gentle noise — quality leather is audible.
  • No novelty or cartoon socks for formal events.
  • Jeans should fit — not too baggy nor too tight.
  • Tuck or untuck shirt appropriately — neatness beats “rules.”

Communication & Behavior (31-50)

  • Listen more than you talk — presence matters.
  • Make eye contact — shows confidence and respect.
  • Greet people with a firm handshake.
  • Write thank-you notes — gratitude is classy.
  • Pay attention to table manners.
  • Answer calls/messages promptly — respect others’ time.
  • Don’t interrupt — polite restraint projects confidence.
  • Ask questions — it shows engagement.
  • Avoid gossip — it undermines credibility.
  • Apologize sincerely when wrong.
  • Manage digital etiquette — mute before entering rooms.
  • Don’t dominate group conversations.
  • Speak thoughtfully — volume ≠ strength.
  • Adjust tone to context — formal vs casual.
  • Don’t overshare personal finances or troubles.
  • Keep personal complaints private.
  • Compliment genuinely, not flippantly.
  • Use humor wisely — inclusive, not mean-spirited.
  • Stay calm under stress — collect your thoughts before reacting.
  • Respect boundaries — physical and verbal.

Lifestyle & Habits (51-75)

  • Own quality luggage — travel with poise.
  • Plan your week — time management is mastery.
  • Read daily — knowledge builds depth.
  • Cook a signature meal — hospitality is rare.
  • Always be learning — skills keep you relevant.
  • Pay bills on time — responsibility earns peace.
  • Keep a clean inbox — digital clutter drains.
  • Invest in rest — burnout kills productivity.
  • Protect your posture even when tired.
  • Practice mindfulness — focus improves decisions.
  • Learn basic self-defense — preparedness builds confidence.
  • Manage money — savings protect freedom.
  • Dress like you expect opportunity.
  • Be punctual — time respect is life respect.
  • Stay hydrated — clear head, better body.
  • Build a capsule wardrobe — versatile pieces mix smarter.
  • Replace old items before they embarrass you.
  • Invest in skincare — healthy skin looks sharp.
  • Clean glasses/sunglasses frequently.
  • Backup important data & passwords.
  • Know basic repairs (sew a button, tie a knot).
  • Avoid trends that don’t suit your style.
  • Learn how to iron and launder properly.
  • Update wardrobe seasonally — light fabrics in heat.
  • Store clothes properly — hang, fold, protect.

Relationships & Respect (76-100)

  • Be generous with kindness.
  • Offer help before being asked.
  • Respect others’ time — don’t make them wait.
  • Listen actively with empathy.
  • Give space when needed.
  • Communicate expectations clearly.
  • Don’t make assumptions — clarify.
  • Acknowledge others’ successes.
  • Be dependable in small things first.
  • Seek respect, not attention.
  • Be on time for personal plans too.
  • Hold doors and offer coats — timeless courtesy.
  • Offer genuine compliments — not flattery.
  • Respond to conflict with calm solutions.
  • Spend quality time with loved ones.
  • Acknowledge effort, not just outcomes.
  • Say “please” and “thank you” sincerely.
  • Offer help before advice.
  • Keep confidences — trust is sacred.
  • Be loyal, but not blind to faults.
  • Don’t criticize in anger.
  • Respect elders and learn from them.
  • Encourage others’ growth.
  • Protect others’ dignity in disagreements.
  • Live by your values — consistency builds trust.
