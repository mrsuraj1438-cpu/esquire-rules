Esquire-Style: Rules 1–10

Self-Respect & Confidence

  • 1. Stand tall — good posture speaks confidence.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: Posture reflects self-assurance and attracts respect.
    How: Keep your back straight, shoulders back, head up.
    Apply: Practice standing tall every morning and when meeting new people.
  • 2. Keep your word — reliability builds trust.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: People respect those who honor commitments.
    How: Only promise what you can deliver and follow through.
    Apply: Track tasks and meet deadlines consistently.
  • 3. Never beg for validation.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: Seeking approval appears insecure.
    How: Focus on your own assessment, not others’ opinions.
    Apply: Celebrate achievements privately and confidently.
  • 4. Speak clearly — mumbling undermines authority.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: Clear speech conveys confidence and professionalism.
    How: Practice articulating words, avoid filler sounds.
    Apply: Speak slowly in meetings or conversations, making your point clear.
  • 5. Own at least one well-tailored suit.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: A good suit projects sophistication and readiness.
    How: Invest in a suit that fits your body properly.
    Apply: Wear it for interviews, events, or important occasions.
  • 6. Shine your shoes — details are noticed.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: People notice small details that reflect personal care.
    How: Regularly polish shoes and maintain them.
    Apply: Set a routine to check and shine shoes before events.
  • 7. Stay active — don’t skip the gym.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: Physical fitness improves confidence, energy, and presence.
    How: Schedule workouts and stick to them.
    Apply: Create a weekly workout routine and track progress.
  • 8. Avoid oversharing online.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: Oversharing can compromise privacy and perception.
    How: Think before posting personal info or complaints.
    Apply: Post selectively, maintain a professional or neutral online image.
  • 9. Control your intoxication — dignity first.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: Overindulgence can harm reputation and health.
    How: Drink responsibly and know your limits.
    Apply: Avoid peer pressure, always plan safe transportation.
  • 10. Keep your home presentable for guests.
    Why / How / Apply
    Why: A clean home reflects personal discipline and respect.
    How: Regularly tidy, declutter, and maintain living spaces.
    Apply: Establish a cleaning schedule and maintain organization.
